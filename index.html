Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Information and Communication Engineering at University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). I am currently a visiting student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.

My research focuses on deep learning algorithms for optimization in edge computing networks. My interests include edge intelligence, mobile edge computing, computing power networks, resource allocation, and privacy protection in dynamic network environments.
